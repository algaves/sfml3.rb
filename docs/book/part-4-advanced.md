# Part IV — Advanced

Cameras, shaders, particles, event-driven audio, architecture and interface. These recipes combine the earlier pieces into the systems a larger game needs; several are grouped into their own multi-page sections.

1. **[Views]({% link book/views.md %})** &middot; *Advanced* — The camera rectangle, split-screen viewports, parallax and scrolling.
2. **[Shaders & Vertex Arrays]({% link book/shaders.md %})** &middot; *Advanced* — GLSL programs and raw vertex geometry on the GPU.
3. **[Particle Systems]({% link book/particles.md %})** &middot; *Intermediate* — Emitters, velocity, gravity, lifetime and fade.
4. **[Music & Event-Triggered Audio]({% link book/music-triggers.md %})** &middot; *Advanced* — Looping music plus sound effects fired by gameplay events.
5. **[Triggers, Events & ECS]({% link book/ecs.md %})** &middot; *Advanced* — A minimal entity-component-system with trigger areas.
6. **[GUI]({% link book/gui.md %})** &middot; *Advanced* — Immediate-mode menus, a HUD, a logo and a splash screen.
7. **[Networking]({% link book/networking.md %})** &middot; *Advanced* — Sockets, packets, TCP, UDP and file transfer.
